# Qualification — Project Buddy

## Qualification Criteria
1. **Business Fit (Fit Score 0-100)**: Industry alignment, company maturity, technical need, decision-maker accessibility, likelihood of conversion.
2. **Buying Intent (Intent Score 0-100)**: Explicit project requirement (+30), relevant hiring (+20), expansion (+15), new product (+15), clear operational friction (+10), lead opportunity (+10), modernization signal (+10), decision maker found (+10). Deductions: no current signal (-20), unverified assumption (-10).
3. **Thresholds**: Minimum default intent = 70, minimum default fit = 70.
