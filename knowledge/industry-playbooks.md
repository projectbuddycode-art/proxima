# Industry Playbooks — Project Buddy

## Lighting Manufacturers & Showrooms
- **Friction Points**: Large product catalogues presented via non-searchable PDF catalogues; quotes handled manually over WhatsApp; high lead drop-off.
- **Recommended Offer**: Premium Digital Lighting Showroom with WhatsApp RFQ flow & qualification.

## Electrical & EPC Contractors
- **Friction Points**: Project portfolio updated rarely; complex technical RFQ process with delayed estimate turnarounds.
- **Recommended Offer**: Digital Business Development System with automated estimation request intake.

## Marketing & Software Agencies
- **Friction Points**: Client demand for custom web apps, AI integrations, or CRMs exceeding internal dev bandwidth.
- **Recommended Offer**: Technical Execution Partnership (White-label software delivery).
