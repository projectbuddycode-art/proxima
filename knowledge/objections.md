# Objections — Project Buddy

## Handling Common Objections
- **"We already have an in-house team / agency"**: Acknowledge their existing setup. Position Project Buddy as overflow execution or specialized AI/automation layer rather than a replacement.
- **"What is the cost?"**: Explain that pricing depends on bottleneck scope and measurable outcome, offering a brief 15-minute discovery call to map the problem before discussing numbers.
- **"Send us a deck / brochure"**: Provide a brief 3-bullet breakdown tailored specifically to their situation rather than sending a generic PDF.
