# Messaging Rules — Project Buddy

## Forbidden Phrases (Strictly Prohibited)
- "We are an AI agency."
- "We build websites."
- "We provide AI solutions."
- "We would love to help."
- "Can we schedule a meeting?"
- "Would you be interested in our services?"
- "Dear Sir/Madam."
- "Just following up."
- "Hope you are doing well."
- "Unlock the power of AI."
- "Take your business to the next level."

## Required Message Structure
1. **Specific Observation**: Point out a specific observable detail about their website, product catalogue, hiring, or expansion.
2. **Business Implication / Hypothesis**: Frame how that detail might create operational friction, lead drop-off, or delays.
3. **One Thoughtful Question**: Ask a single natural, low-friction question aimed at starting a conversation.

The primary objective of the first message is to get a **REPLY**, not a sale, proposal, or meeting request.
