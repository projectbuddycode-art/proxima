# Commercial Logic — Project Buddy

## Commercial Strategy & Pricing Principles
- **Directional Bands Only**: Internal commercial bands are directional estimates (e.g. Small Modernization: $2k-$5k; Mid-Tier System: $5k-$15k; Enterprise Suite: $15k+).
- **Never Quote Early**: Never present guaranteed prices or ranges to prospects without completing thorough discovery and scope definition.
- **Value Framing**: Calculate commercial value based on bottleneck cost savings, lead conversion uplift, or manual hour elimination.
