# Sales Methodology — Project Buddy

## The 13-Step Commercial Sequence
1. RESEARCH
2. BUYING INTENT
3. PAIN HYPOTHESIS
4. BUSINESS IMPACT
5. COMMERCIAL ANGLE
6. PERSONALIZED MESSAGE
7. CONVERSATION
8. QUALIFICATION
9. DISCOVERY
10. MEETING
11. PROPOSAL
12. NEGOTIATION
13. CLOSE

## Non-Negotiable Rule
Never skip directly from "company found" to "Project Buddy can build an AI system for you." Always establish:
- What is happening inside this company?
- What changed?
- What appears inefficient?
- What does that inefficiency potentially cost?
- Why might they care now?
- What Project Buddy capability best addresses it?
- What question would naturally start a conversation?
