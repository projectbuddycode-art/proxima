# Follow-Up Methodology — Project Buddy

## Outreach Cadence
- **DAY 0**: Initial outreach (Observation → Implication → Question).
- **DAY 2**: Value-added follow-up (Sharing a relevant insight or specific workflow observation).
- **DAY 5**: Case study / useful mini-framework (No fluff, high utility).
- **DAY 8**: Soft final follow-up (Low-friction closing note).

## Rules
- NEVER say "Just following up" or "Checking in".
- Every follow-up MUST introduce a new reason or insight.
- If prospect replies or unsubscribes: INSTANTLY STOP THE FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE.
