# Offers Knowledge — Project Buddy

## Core Reusable Offers

### 1. Digital Lighting Showroom / Experience
- **Target**: Retailers, Design Brands, Manufacturers, EPC Contractors.
- **Workflow**: Product discovery → visual presentation → product details → WhatsApp enquiry → RFQ/consultation → optional CRM qualification.
- **Nomenclature per Industry**:
  - Retailer: *Premium Digital Showroom*
  - Brand: *Digital Lighting Experience*
  - Manufacturer: *Digital Product Catalogue + B2B Lead Generation*
  - Electrical / EPC: *Digital Business Development System*

### 2. Operational Modernization & Automation Suite
- **Target**: Mid-market businesses with manual enquiry drop-off or repetitive back-office workflow bottlenecks.
- **Components**: Lead qualification routing, automated status updates, CRM integration, invoice/quoting pipeline.

### 3. Technical Execution Partnership
- **Target**: Agencies, Consultants, ERP/CRM implementations partners, Recruiters.
- **Positioning**: Partner retains strategic client relationship; Project Buddy provides white-label technical execution for custom software, AI integration, CRM/ERP, and dashboards.
