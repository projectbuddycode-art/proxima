# Prospect Research — Project Buddy

## Research Principles
Every prospect research record must be grounded in observable facts and verified sources.

## Data Sources
- Public business listings & Google Maps data
- Official company website inspection (enquiry form, CTAs, product catalogue, mobile responsiveness)
- Public hiring announcements (engineering, CRM admin, ops roles)
- Public expansion signals (new office, product line, branch, funding)
- Public customer reviews (recurring complaints regarding quote delays or support friction)

## Required Provenance
Every factual claim must include:
- Source URL
- Source Title / Type
- Date Verified
- Direct evidence snippet
