# Meeting Preparation — Project Buddy

## Discovery Meeting Agenda
1. **Understand Business & Current Workflow**: How do leads enter? How are quotes created? Where does manual delay occur?
2. **Quantify Bottleneck Cost**: Hours lost per week, missed enquiries, conversion drop-off percentage.
3. **Identify Target Outcome**: Speed to quote, automated customer qualification, centralized tracking.
4. **Determine Technical Scope & Solution**: Recommend simplest effective solution (custom web app, CRM workflow, AI classifier, digital showroom).
